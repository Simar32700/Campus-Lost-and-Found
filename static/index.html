How CampusFinder works

  1. Report your item. Add its description, location, date, and a photo if you have one.
  2. Let us find a match. Similar lost and found reports go to a campus administrator for review.
  3. Coordinate the return. Once a match is approved, use Messages to arrange the handover.
  4. Need more help? Follow up through Complaints after a dismissed chat, or email our team for account and website support.
